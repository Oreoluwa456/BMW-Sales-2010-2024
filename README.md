# BMW Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

The **BMW Sales Analysis** is an Excel-based data analytics project focused on understanding BMW sales performance across different vehicle characteristics and markets.

Using a dataset covering **2010–2024**, the analysis examines sales volume, revenue, regions, fuel types, BMW models, vehicle colors, transmission types, mileage, engine size, and annual sales value.

The project uses Pivot Tables and summary metrics to turn raw automotive sales data into clear business insights that can support sales, marketing, and inventory decisions.

## Problem Statement

Raw automotive sales data can be difficult to interpret when information is spread across different vehicle attributes and markets. Without structured analysis, it can be challenging to identify which regions, models, fuel types, and vehicle characteristics contribute most to sales.

This project addresses this by organizing the BMW sales data into key performance areas and presenting the findings through an Excel dashboard.

## Business Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Evaluate overall BMW sales volume and revenue.
- Identify the strongest-performing regions.
- Compare sales performance across fuel types.
- Identify the top-performing BMW models.
- Understand customer preferences by vehicle color.
- Compare Automatic and Manual transmission sales.
- Analyze annual sales value.
- Examine average mileage and engine size.

## Dataset Information

**Dataset:** BMW Sales Dataset
- https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ayeshaseherr/bmw-dataset

**Time Period:** 2010–2024

**Industry:** Automotive

**Key Metrics:** Sales Volume, Sales Quantity, Price, Revenue, Mileage, Engine Size

**Key Dimensions:** Year, Region, Fuel Type, Model, Color, Transmission

## Key Performance Indicators

**Average Sales Volume:** **5.1K**

**Average Revenue:** **$75.0K**

**Average Mileage:** **10.0K KM**

**Average Engine Size:** **3L**

**Total Sales Volume:** **253.4M**

## Key Analysis

### Fuel Type Performance

**Hybrid** recorded the highest sales volume at **64.5M**, followed by **Petrol at 63.3M**, **Electric at 63.2M**, and **Diesel at 62.4M**.

The relatively small difference between the categories indicates that sales volume is fairly balanced across the four fuel types.

### Regional Performance

**Asia** recorded the highest sales volume at **43.0M**, followed by **Europe at 42.6M** and **North America at 42.4M**.

The **Middle East** recorded **42.3M**, while **Africa** and **South America** both recorded approximately **41.6M**.

### Top 5 BMW Models

The five highest-performing models in the analysis were:

- **7 Series:** 23.8M
- **i8:** 23.4M
- **X1:** 23.4M
- **3 Series:** 23.3M
- **i3:** 23.1M

The **7 Series** recorded the highest sales volume among the five models at **23.8M**.

### Color Performance

**Red** recorded the highest sales volume at **42.8M**, closely followed by **Silver at 42.7M**.

Other colors included:

- **White:** 42.3M
- **Blue:** 42.0M
- **Grey:** 42.0M
- **Black:** 41.7M

This shows relatively balanced sales across the analyzed vehicle colors.

### Transmission Performance

**Manual vehicles:** **127.4M**

**Automatic vehicles:** **126.0M**

Manual transmission vehicles recorded a slightly higher sales volume, although the difference between the two categories is relatively small.

### Sales Value by Year

The annual analysis shows that **2022 recorded the highest total Price_USD value at $260.7M**, followed by **2024 at $257.1M** and **2019 at $255.1M**.

The **lowest value was recorded in 2020 at $240.5M**.

The results show a notable increase from **$254.2M in 2021 to $260.7M in 2022**, followed by a decrease to **$257.1M in 2024**.

This analysis is based on the **Sum of Price_USD** measure in the Pivot Table.

## Technical Approach

The analysis was completed using a structured Excel workflow:

- Reviewed and organized the BMW sales dataset.
- Created Pivot Tables for different business dimensions.
- Calculated summary KPIs such as average sales volume, revenue, mileage, and engine size.
- Compared sales performance across regions, fuel types, models, colors, and transmission types.
- Analyzed annual Price_USD values to identify the highest and lowest-performing years.
- Used Excel dashboard visualizations to present the results in a clear and accessible format.
